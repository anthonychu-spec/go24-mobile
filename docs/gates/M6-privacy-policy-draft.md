## 2. What the App is for

The App lets members of our clubs sign in, see their membership and member pass, book and cancel classes, see their visit history, and receive notifications about their bookings. Your membership itself is held in our club management system and is not created or cancelled through the App.

## 3. Personal data we collect

**From our club management system (PerfectGym, "PGM"), when you sign in.** We check your email and password, or a one-time code sent to your email, with PGM. We read your member ID, member number, name, email, phone number, membership status and expiry, and visit history. We store your member ID, member number, email and phone number. Your name, membership details and visit history are read when you open the relevant screen and are not stored by the App's server.

**When you use the App.**
- Class bookings and cancellations: class, club, date and time, and booking status. Bookings are also recorded in PGM.
- App settings, such as reminder time, and classes you mark as favourites.
- Notifications we send you, kept in the App's inbox.
- Sign-in sessions: an identifier for your device, the platform (iOS or Android), which app you signed in through, and when the session was last used.
- A push notification token, if you allow notifications.

**Automatically.** Our servers keep logs of requests (including IP address, time, the address requested and errors) for security and troubleshooting. [If crash reporting is enabled: when the App or server has an error, a diagnostic report is sent to our crash-reporting provider. These reports do not include your name, email or phone number.]

**We do not collect** your location, contacts, photos, health data or advertising identifiers. We do not show advertising, do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and do not sell personal data.

## 4. How we use it

- To confirm you are a member and keep you signed in.
- To show your membership, member pass, bookings and visits.
- To book and cancel classes for you in PGM.
- To send notifications you have allowed, such as booking confirmations and class reminders.
- To keep the service secure, prevent abuse (for example, limiting repeated sign-in attempts) and fix problems.

We use your data only for these purposes, or for a directly related purpose. We will ask for your consent before using it for anything else, including direct marketing.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Your App account data (section 3) is kept until you delete your account.
- Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days without use.
- Server logs are deleted after 30 days.
- Backups are deleted after 30 days, so deleted data may remain in a backup for up to 30 days.
- [Payment records are kept for 7 years to meet tax and accounting requirements. The THE LAB app does not take payments.]
- After you delete your account, booking records are kept for statistics with your identity removed.

## 7. Deleting your account

You can delete your App account at any time:
- **In the App:** Profile → Delete account.
- **On the web**, even if you no longer have the App: [https://API-DOMAIN/v1/account-deletion?brand=lab]. Enter your member email; we send you a one-time code to confirm it is you.

One account is shared by all our member apps (THE LAB by ONYX, GO24 Fitness, ONYX by GO24, The Foundry). Deleting it signs you out of all of them and deletes your sessions, devices, push token, notifications, settings and favourites. Booking records are kept with your identity removed.

**Deleting your App account does not cancel your membership** and does not stop membership fees. Your membership record stays in PGM. Classes you have already booked are not cancelled; cancel them in the App before deleting, or contact the front desk. To cancel your membership, contact your club's front desk.

You may sign in again later with your member email. This creates a new, empty App account.

## 9. Security
